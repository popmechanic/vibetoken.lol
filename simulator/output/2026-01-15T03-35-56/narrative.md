## Simulation Results: Startup + Mixed Behaviors

This analysis covers 100 simulations of a startup business model over 36 months.

**Quick Summary:**
- Business survival rate: 63%
- Mean final token price: $1.82
- Revenue share (α): 20%
- Initial participant count: 5

### Business Outcomes

The startup achieved breakthrough in 63% of simulations, exceeding typical startup success rates. Failed businesses (37%) typically exhausted their runway before finding product-market fit.

**Revenue Distribution:**
- Mean: $89,628
- Median: $29,175
- Range: $287 - $682,006

The significant gap between mean and median revenue indicates a right-skewed distribution with occasional extreme successes.

**Behavior Analysis:**

**Diamond hands outperformed** in this scenario, achieving 6.80x average ROI. Their refusal to exit captured full upside when the business succeeded.

**Rational actors** achieved 6.01x average ROI. Their conservative approach may have triggered premature exits in some successful scenarios.

**Trend followers** achieved 7.49x average ROI. Momentum-based decisions led to variable outcomes depending on price trajectory timing.

**Skeptics** achieved 7.28x average ROI. Their early exits may have left money on the table in successful scenarios.



### Economic Observations

**Token Price Dynamics:**
- Final price range: $1.44 - $2.63
- Coefficient of variation: 13.6%

The square root pricing function (P = k×√S) delivered relatively stable prices despite varying revenue conditions. A 14% coefficient of variation suggests the bonding curve effectively dampened volatility.

**Supply Floor (S_min = 1000):**
The minimum supply floor prevented price collapse in failure scenarios. Floor price: $0.32. No simulation reached the floor, indicating adequate supply cushion.

**Exit Queue Mechanism:**
In failed scenarios, exit queues froze when revenue stopped. Participants who exited early captured value; late exits risked queue freeze.

### Key Findings

1. **Mixed outcomes favor balanced strategies.** Neither pure conviction nor pure caution dominated. Risk management mattered.

2. **Price Chaser #5** achieved highest returns (15.02x), while **Angel Referrer** achieved lowest (6.01x).

3. **The tokenomics performed as designed.** Supply-based pricing provided price discovery, distributions rewarded holders, and the exit queue enabled liquidity without secondary markets.

**Implication:** For startup businesses with similar parameters, the token system creates meaningful value for early contributors.
