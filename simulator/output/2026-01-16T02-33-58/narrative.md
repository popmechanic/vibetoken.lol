## Simulation Results: Micro Business + Diamond Hands

This analysis covers 20 simulations of a micro business model over 36 months.

**Quick Summary:**
- Business survival rate: 95%
- Mean final token price: $2.00
- Revenue share (α): 20%

Despite high revenue volatility, the micro business survived in 95% of cases. The square root pricing function helped dampen price swings during spiky revenue periods.

### Economic Observations

**Token Price Dynamics:**
- Final price range: $1.05 - $16.23
- Coefficient of variation: 163.6%

Price variation of 164% tracked the underlying business risk. The bonding curve damped but didn't eliminate swings—appropriate for this volatility profile.

**Supply Floor (S_min = 1000):**
The minimum supply floor prevented price collapse in failure scenarios. Floor price: $0.25. No simulation reached the floor, indicating adequate supply cushion.

**Exit Queue Mechanism:**
With high survival rate, exit queues typically cleared quickly. The FIFO queue processed exits from ongoing revenue without delays.

### Key Findings

1. **High survival rate rewards patience.** Diamond hands and long-term holders outperformed reactive strategies in this scenario.

2. **Early Adopter** achieved highest returns (2766.37x), while **Committed Holder** achieved lowest (1051.28x).

### Design Principle Check

- ✓ **Early contributor reward**: 2423.4x ROI for month 1-2 entrants vs 1051.3x for later (2.3x advantage)
- ✓ **Bounded volatility**: Revenue CV 434% → Price CV 164% (62% dampening)
- ✓ **Floor protection**: No runs hit S_min
- ✓ **Exit liquidity**: High survival enabled queue clearance without secondary markets

**Implication:** For micro businesses with similar parameters, the token system creates meaningful value for early contributors.
