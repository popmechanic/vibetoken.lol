**Behavior Analysis:**

**Diamond hands showed mixed results.** While they captured upside in successful scenarios, they also bore full losses in failures. Average ROI: 1378.18x.

**Rational actors** achieved 7.69x average ROI. Their NPV-based exit decisions helped preserve capital in the majority of failing scenarios.

**Trend followers** achieved 2897.34x average ROI. Momentum-based decisions led to variable outcomes depending on price trajectory timing.

**Skeptics** achieved 7.98x average ROI. Their caution was rewarded—early exits preserved capital when businesses failed to validate.



### Economic Observations

**Token Price Dynamics:**
- Final price range: $1.27 - $3.38
- Coefficient of variation: 24.0%

The square root pricing function (P = k×√S) delivered relatively stable prices despite varying revenue conditions. A 24% coefficient of variation suggests the bonding curve effectively dampened volatility.

**Supply Floor (S_min = 1000):**
The minimum supply floor prevented price collapse in failure scenarios. Floor price: $0.25. No simulation reached the floor, indicating adequate supply cushion.

**Exit Queue Mechanism:**
In failed scenarios, exit queues froze when revenue stopped. Participants who exited early captured value; late exits risked queue freeze.

### Key Findings

1. **Mixed outcomes favor balanced strategies.** Neither pure conviction nor pure caution dominated. Risk management mattered.

2. **Friend of Hustler #16** achieved highest returns (30957.75x), while **Early Exit Emily** achieved lowest (7.43x).

3. **The tokenomics performed as designed.** Supply-based pricing provided price discovery, distributions rewarded holders, and the exit queue enabled liquidity without secondary markets.

**Implication:** Contributors should factor in business risk—the exit queue can freeze when revenue stops, stranding tokens without liquidity.
