## Simulation Results: Micro Business + Diamond Hands

This analysis covers 100 simulations of a micro business model over 36 months.

**Quick Summary:**
- Business survival rate: 94%
- Mean final token price: $1.39
- Revenue share (α): 20%
- Initial participant count: 3

### Business Outcomes

Despite high revenue volatility, the micro business survived in 94% of cases. The square root pricing function helped dampen price swings during spiky revenue periods.

**Revenue Distribution:**
- Mean: $1,912,513
- Median: $66,676
- Range: $1,827 - $58,505,402

The significant gap between mean and median revenue indicates a right-skewed distribution with occasional extreme successes.

**Behavior Analysis:**

**Diamond hands outperformed** in this scenario, achieving 106.25x average ROI. Their refusal to exit captured full upside when the business succeeded.



### Economic Observations

**Token Price Dynamics:**
- Final price range: $1.05 - $2.21
- Coefficient of variation: 28.8%

The square root pricing function (P = k×√S) delivered relatively stable prices despite varying revenue conditions. A 29% coefficient of variation suggests the bonding curve effectively dampened volatility.

**Supply Floor (S_min = 1000):**
The minimum supply floor prevented price collapse in failure scenarios. Floor price: $0.25. No simulation reached the floor, indicating adequate supply cushion.

**Exit Queue Mechanism:**
With high survival rate, exit queues typically cleared quickly. The FIFO queue processed exits from ongoing revenue without delays.

### Key Findings

1. **High survival rate rewards patience.** Diamond hands and long-term holders outperformed reactive strategies in this scenario.

2. **Early Adopter** achieved highest returns (140.16x), while **Committed Holder** achieved lowest (87.20x).

3. **The tokenomics performed as designed.** Supply-based pricing provided price discovery, distributions rewarded holders, and the exit queue enabled liquidity without secondary markets.

**Implication:** For micro businesses with similar parameters, the token system creates meaningful value for early contributors.
